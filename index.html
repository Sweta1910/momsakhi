About MomSakhi

A sakhi for every stage of motherhood.

Share anything. No judgment. You don't have to carry it alone.

MomSakhi is a moderated community where moms share, ask for advice and support each other. You can post anonymously whenever you need to — other moms see “Anonymous mom” instead of your name. MomSakhi is in open beta: any mom can join by signing in with Google.
